Three pieces of work

01

Gaming ads, from daily features to realtime.

Homepage decisions could not wait overnight. User, item, app, material, and UGC features became realtime, attribution was made accurate, the path was extended from Android to iOS, and the design landed on the IEM feature platform so recommendation could reuse it.

Homepage effect up more than 2%. Install and iOS features stopped being one-off projects.

02

A lakehouse move, with the risk kept in dual-run.

MaxCompute had to be left without breaking ads or training. The target was Spark on a lake. On DataWorks, EMR Serverless Spark, and DLF, 900+ tables entered dual-run: sync, translation, validation, auth, and long-job stability closed on one path.

Some BI went from about 75 seconds to 200 milliseconds. Hologres moved from 2.2 to 4.x, ahead of product EOS.

03

The engines were modified, not merely operated.

At Vipshop I owned the Hadoop engine on 800+ nodes, including source-level tuning and hot upgrades. At Pinduoduo I worked on Flink 1.5 / 1.8: hot restart, hot update, and checkpoints. At Agora I shipped an in-house Flink distribution, 900+ community patches, and moved a log path peaking near 20 million QPS from Spark onto Flink.

TapTap used that same judgment. Not a product brochure.
